# Nine current acceptance gates

All are mandatory for full academy approval. PARTIAL, REVIEW and NOT_TESTED never count as PASS.

1. Curriculum: all 15 role roadmaps mapped to substantial beginner/intermediate/advanced teaching and prerequisites.
2. Content: explanation, examples, guided and independent practice, mistakes and revision; qualified accuracy review.
3. Resources: each external resource/video checked for purpose, accessibility and reuse rights, with a review date.
4. Assessments: independent answer-key review, adequate varied questions and practical rubrics; completion separated from mastery.
5. Career: role-specific project, CV and interview evidence; actual vacancies and official eligibility information checked individually.
6. Progress: full state and auxiliary-record export/import/undo, malformed/oversized file rejection, quota rollback and physical-device transfer.
7. Interface: routes and key workflows, narrow layouts, keyboard, labels, screen reader, zoom and error recovery.
8. Offline: real secure-origin install, restart while disconnected, updates and retained progress on iPhone and laptop.
9. Security/privacy: no secrets or real patient data, safe imported data and escaped output; independent review of the complete app.

Current evidence is in reports/V51-*.json. Full release acceptance is pending. Earlier gate definitions and results are historical.
