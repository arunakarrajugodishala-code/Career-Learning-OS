## Short-video selection

All 36 existing resource groups receive an added short option, covering the 223 mapped lesson/activity/scenario titles. This is assignment coverage, not unique exact-topic film coverage. Existing full links are retained. Selected short overviews: Fireship SQL, Python, Git and machine learning; W3C accessibility; ICO's three-minute data protection introduction; Medblocks FHIR. Other resources are bounded five-minute excerpts from the existing video, explicitly labelled. Interview/English use the British Council scene and transcript. All loading is user-initiated; no autoplay. External videos require internet and may restrict embedding, region or sign-in. HTTP success does not establish playback, caption quality, licence rights or full educational coverage. Older privacy videos must be read alongside current ICO guidance.

## Career preparation scope

15 routes, ordered existing course links, role-specific portfolio briefs, three technical/project model responses plus two common interview structures per route. Eight original AI learning steps include explanation, example, practical task, evidence rubric and interview reasoning. CV workshop preserves user evidence, flags missing criterion evidence and exports a text draft. No automatic ATS ranking, fabricated experience, automatic submission or shortlist guarantee. Job searches are UK NHS/DWP and international LinkedIn with location filters set on the destination site. Optional certificate choices are based on provider content and role fit, not measured employer demand in a user's location. Some routes prioritise practical work rather than a generic certificate.
